Five more plugins have had pull requests open on the community repo since October 27: Show Tag, Pronouns, Hide Call Buttons, Staff Tags and Live Markdown. Since I'm not sure when they'll be reviewed, I've decided to share them here in the meantime. Details on Current plugins.

Why it exists

I've got a lot of plugins and don't want to flood the community repo with ones that aren't ready. They land here first once they work and are safe to use, and I keep polishing them. Plugins here work, but expect rough edges and frequent updates.

Most plugins here are ones I plan to contribute to the community repo once it meets their standard. Plugins that will stay unofficial, like AI Core and Pocket Browser, will be listed separately.

Account safety

No plugin reads your token, sends its own requests to Discord, retries after Discord says no, or does anything on your account without you asking.
