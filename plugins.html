Current plugins

In community review

I've had these five pull requests open on the community repo since October 27. Since I'm not sure when they'll be reviewed, I've decided to go ahead and share them here in the meantime. If any of them get merged later on, the community version will take over, and I'll update this page to let you know.

Show Tag

Usernames next to display names in chat.

  • The username after the display name in message headers and reply previews
  • Or replace the display name with the username

Pronouns

People's pronouns as a small tag next to their name in chat.

  • From Discord profiles, server profiles and PronounDB (PronounDB can be turned off)
  • Only real pronouns by default, so bios and jokes in the field don't become a badge
  • One colour for everyone, their role colour, or a colour per person
  • Set or override anyone's pronouns yourself

Hide Call Buttons

Hides the call and video buttons you keep tapping by mistake.

  • The phone and camera icons at the top of DMs and group DMs
  • The call button on profiles and in the friends list
  • The camera button in voice channel controls
  • Each one has its own switch

Staff Tags

OWNER, ADMIN, STAFF, MOD, VC Mod, Chat Mod and WEBHOOK tags, like old desktop plugins.

  • In chat, on profiles and in member lists
  • Change each tag's text, colour, gradient or icon, or show just the icon
  • Optionally colour tags with the person's top role

Live Markdown Android only

Formatting shows up in the message box as you type.

  • Bold, italics, underline, strikethrough, code, spoilers, headings, subtext and ANSI colours styled as you type
  • Optionally fades the symbols and grows headings like in chat
  • Select text to get formatting buttons next to Copy and Paste

Batch one

The seven plugins that opened the repo. All JavaScript only, so they work on Android and iOS.

Most plugins have a changelog: the clock icon at the top right of their settings.

Custom Timestamps

Choose how message times look.

  • Calendar ("Today at 14:05"), relative ("5 minutes ago"), ISO 8601 or your own format
  • Optionally shows name, avatar and time on every message instead of grouping

Hide Servers

Hide what you don't want to see, on your device only.

  • Hide servers, folders and channels from their long-press menus
  • Bring anything back from settings
  • You stay in the server, and other devices aren't affected

New Members

A sprout next to people who joined in the last week, like desktop.

  • Badge as an emoji, a recoloured icon or Discord's own
  • Any colour, or reset to green

No Dividers

Removes the thin lines between rows in settings and lists.

  • Nothing to set up; turn it off to bring them back

ReviewDB

Read and write reviews on profiles and servers.

  • Reviews on user profiles and in server menus
  • Write, vote on, delete and report reviews
  • Block reviewers; hide warnings or timestamps
  • Notifications for new reviews of you
  • Sign in with Discord's own Authorize screen (ReviewDB only sees your username and avatar)

Send Tweaks

Tidies messages as you send them.

  • Strips tracking from links (YouTube, Spotify, X, Instagram, Amazon…) plus ClearURLs rules
  • Capitalise sentences, add apostrophes, end with a full stop
  • Your own find-and-replace rules for text or links (e.g. X → fxtwitter), optionally live as you type
  • Dates, times and snippets inside replacements
  • Silent messages, always or just once
  • Hold send and swipe up to send one message silent, loud or exactly as typed
  • Optionally works on edits; import and export your rules

Timezones

See someone's local time next to their messages.

  • Set anyone's timezone by searching a place or picking from a list
  • Shows their time, and optionally their day, in 12- or 24-hour
  • Reads the shared timezone list other clients use (can be turned off)

Batch two

Three of the five have native Android code, so they're Android only.

Round Corners

Undoes the redesign's squares: round servers, round buttons, a pill You bar.

  • Round server icons, and round Home, Create and Discover buttons next to them
  • The You bar as a pill with round buttons; the message box and chat bar buttons round too
  • Each part can be turned on or off on its own

Sharp Corners Android only

The opposite: every corner in Discord sharp.

  • Buttons, the message box, the You bar, menus, cards, sheets, server icons and pictures
  • The chat too: embeds, attachments, reactions, voice messages, mentions
  • Optionally circles as well, so avatars, status dots and round buttons become squares

Text Animation Android only

Letters animate as you type and delete, with a smooth gliding cursor.

  • New letters fade in or grow in with a bounce; pastes ripple in one after another
  • Deleted letters fall away or turn to dust
  • The cursor slides to where you type or tap and fades instead of blinking, with an optional stretchy trail
  • Optionally every text field, not just the message box; password fields never animate
  • Needs Android 10 or newer

Character Counter

Counts your message's characters in a square above the message box, and splits long ones into several.

  • Characters typed, characters left, or typed out of the limit; shown always or only near the limit
  • Past the limit it turns red and says how far over you are
  • Split long messages: sends a long message as several parts, in order, with a limit on how many and a wait between them

My community pull request for this one was closed: another Character Counter was merged first. This is my version, split and all.

Pocket Browser Android only

A web browser with tabs and history in a new tab of the emoji picker.

  • Tabs, private tabs, bookmarks, history, downloads and find in page
  • Links from chats open on the Web tab; share a page, picture or screenshot back to the chat
  • Pop-out video player; videos keep playing with the picker half open
  • Needs Valenced 1.2.4 or newer
